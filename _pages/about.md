My name is Dr. Sandy M. B. Wilson. I am a sports scientist that specialises in sleep, circadian science, and recovery methods. This site will be used as a landing page to update what I am up to in teaching, research, and applied practice. 

I currently work as a Teaching Fellow at the University of Glasgow where I contribute towards postgraduate courses across the College of Medical, Veterinary & Life Sciences. This includes a primary teaching role on the [MSc Sport and Exercise Science and Medicine](https://www.gla.ac.uk/postgraduate/taught/sport-exercise-science-medicine/), and sleep and circadian teaching across different schools.

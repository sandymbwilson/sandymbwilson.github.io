excerpt: 'This cross-sectional study of student-athlete in a sports academy supports previous research showing this population to present with a high prevalence of undesired sleep characteristics. Furthermore, it showed that the frequency of early morning training was a significant predictor of poorer sleep when controlling for other variables, whereas the frequency of evening training, and both academic- and sport-related time demands were not. This contests the notion that the student-athlete workload restricts sleep opportunity but instead it may be the placement of activities that is important.'

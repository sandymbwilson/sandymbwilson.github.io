I currently work in the Graduate School within MVLS as a Teaching Fellow across sport science and sleep science

In the 2026/27 I am the co-ordinator for the following courses:
- Factors Influencing Performance [MED5361] - MSc Sport and Exercise Science and Medicine, on-campus
- Factors Influencing Performance [MED5348] - MSc Sport and Exercise Science and Medicine, online distance learning
- Sleep and Circadian Timing [PSYCH5116] (co-lead) - Msc Psychology (Conversion), online distance learning
I also provide teaching in most other courses that form the MSc Sport and Exercise Science and Medicine, including the dissertation research project.

I am also involved in scholarship of teaching and learning research, leading [ongoing work](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/393172/) on the use of self-collected data within taught workshops to enhance engagement
